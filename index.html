The beginning of new age

So how do u liked me surpises and i hope this 1/10/2026 will be a unforgettable day for U. 🎂✨ And allah may bless you with endless happiness and love. 🤲❤️ Well now the birthday is almost over and i want to present a small story for my dearest and beautiful your highness. 📖👑

Page 2

Ek ladka jo usually apne phone par scroll karta rehta tha. Ek din Instagram dekhte waqt usse ek notification aayi. 🔔
Usne socha koi random notification hogi, bas red dot clear karne ke liye usne check kiya.

Lekin wahan kisi ne uske comment ka reply kiya tha:

“I want to talk to u.” 💬

Saath mein ek follow request bhi thi.

Ladke ke liye aisa response milna naya tha, isliye usne request accept kar li aur message kiya:

“Hii.” 🙂

Lekin us waqt usse koi response nahi mila, toh usne socha ki shayad iske baare mein zyada bother nahi karna chahiye.

Page 3

Agale din usi person ka uski story par message aaya. Ladke ne reply kiya: 💬

“Hii.”

Aur wahin se unki conversation shuru hui.

Ladki ne poocha:

“Who r u?” 🤔

Ladka sochne laga, “What do you mean? Tum hi toh ho jisne mujhse baat karne ke liye request bheji thi, aur ab tum hi pooch rahi ho ki main kaun hoon?”

Phir ladke ne use yaad dilaya ki usne uske comment ka reply karte hue “I want to talk to u” kaha tha aur follow request bhi bheji thi.

Tab usse yaad aaya. 💭

Page 4

Uske baad unki conversation kaafi smoothly chalne lagi, jaise woh dono pehle se kai baar baat kar chuke hon. ✨

Dheere-dheere unhone ek-doosre ke baare mein kaafi kuch jaana — naam, city, country, favourite food, favourite songs aur bhi bahut si chhoti-chhoti cheezein. 🫶🎶

Ladki ne apni tragic love story bhi dheere-dheere share karni shuru ki. 🥀 Usse trust issues the. Shuru mein usse ladke par bhi doubt tha ki kahin woh koi fraud toh nahi, ya koi aisa person toh nahi jo use jaanta ho aur uske saath prank kar raha ho.

Page 5

Lekin lagbhag ek mahine baad ladke ko uske trust issues ki asli wajah samajh aayi. 💭 Tab usse realize hua ki uska trust karne mein struggle karna genuine tha.

Aur recently uski life mein ek little brother bhi aa gaya — another mother se — aur uske neighbour ne usse confess bhi kar diya. Thoda funny tha, thoda unexpected. 😄✨

Ladka bhi apni kuch stories uske saath share karta raha. 🤝

Pehle month mein unki late-night conversations hoti thi. 🌙💬 Lekin Ramadan shuru hone ke baad ladki busy ho gayi aur unki conversations kaafi kam ho gayi.

Well, woh dono sirf online friends the, aur har kisi ki apni life hoti hai.

Ab unki koi conversation nahi hoti. 🥀

Lekin ladke ke liye woh ek achhi ladki aur ek achhi person thi, aur woh aaj bhi uske liye achha hi wish karta hai. ❤️

Page 6

Is story ki beauty shayad ye thi ki do completely different log — opposite gender, different nations aur different religions se hone ke bawajood — ek short but beautiful friendship bana paaye. 🌍🤝

Ye story sach thi ya nahi, ye sirf reader aur writer jaante hain. 📖

Aur ye friendship bhi kaafi secret thi. 🤫 Ladke ki side se toh kisi ko iske baare mein pata nahi tha. Ladki ki side se kisi ko pata tha ya nahi, ye ladka nahi jaanta.

Agar is poori story ko short mein kehna ho, toh shayad ye kaha ja sakta hai: 💭

Woh dono universe ki marzi se mile the, ek purpose ke liye. 🌌✨ Aur shayad woh purpose bahut pehle hi khatam ho chuka tha. 🥀
